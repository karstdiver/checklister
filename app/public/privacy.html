Privacy Policy

Effective: August 09, 2025

Summary

Your privacy is important to us. This app does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not create hidden backups of your data, and your information is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may collect anonymized data about how you use the app. This information is used solely to improve the app experience and for analytics purposes. All analytics are performed on anonymized, aggregated data and cannot be traced back to any individual user.

Who we are

Checklister (“we”, “us”, “our”). Contact: schecklister@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account: email, display name, authentication UID (via Google/Firebase Auth).
  • App content: checklists, items, item photos you upload.
  • Device/usage: app events, device/model/OS, approximate region (via Firebase Analytics).
  • Security: attestation signals (Firebase App Check) to prevent abuse.

How we use data

  • Provide core features (sign‑in, sync, storage of checklists and photos).
  • Improve reliability and UX (analytics in aggregate).
  • Protect the service (fraud/abuse prevention).

Sharing and processing

  • Processed and stored by Google Firebase services (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Analytics, App Check).
  • We do not sell personal data. Third‑party access is limited to these processors.

Retention and deletion

  • Account/profile persists until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Checklists and photos persist until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Upon account deletion, associated data is deleted within a reasonable period from active systems.

Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS). Access is restricted to authorized systems and personnel.

Children

  • Not directed to children. Users should be of legal age to consent in their region.

Your choices

  • You can delete content in‑app and request account/data deletion via schecklister@gmail.com.

Changes

  • We may update this policy and will post the new effective date here.
