Delete Your Checklister Account

Effective: August 09, 2025

⚠️ Important: Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data including checklists, photos, and account information. This action cannot be undone.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account profile and authentication information
  • All checklists you've created
  • All checklist items and their photos
  • Session history and progress data
  • Any uploaded images and media
  • Account preferences and settings

How to Request Account Deletion

Step-by-Step Process:

1. Contact Support
Send an email to schecklister@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
2. Provide Account Information
Include your email address associated with your Checklister account so we can identify your data
3. Confirm Deletion
We will respond within 48 hours to confirm your request and provide a timeline for deletion
4. Account Removal
Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request

Contact Information

Email: schecklister@gmail.com

Response Time: Within 48 hours

Deletion Timeline: Within 30 days of confirmed request

Alternative: Delete Data Yourself

If you prefer to delete your data manually:

  • Open the Checklister app
  • Go to Settings → Profile
  • Delete individual checklists and photos
  • Note: This doesn't remove your account, only the content

Questions?

If you have questions about account deletion or need help with the process, please contact us at schecklister@gmail.com.
